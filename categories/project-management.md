# Project management

- **[OpenProject](https://www.openproject.org/)** — project planning, timelines, issues, agile boards, and team collaboration.
- **[Taiga](https://taiga.io/)** — agile project management focused on Scrum and Kanban workflows.
- **[Plane](https://plane.so/)** — modern issue and project tracking with a self-hostable community edition.
- **[Leantime](https://leantime.io/)** — project management aimed at small teams and structured planning.

OpenProject is the broadest enterprise-style option; Taiga and Plane are lighter choices for software/product teams.
