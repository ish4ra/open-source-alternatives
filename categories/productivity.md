# Productivity

- **[Joplin](https://joplinapp.org/)** — notes, notebooks, attachments, and synchronization.
- **[Logseq](https://logseq.com/)** — local-first outlining and knowledge management.
- **[Notesnook](https://notesnook.com/)** — cross-platform open-source notes application.
- **[LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/)** — full desktop office suite.
- **[ONLYOFFICE Community](https://www.onlyoffice.com/)** — document editing and collaboration tools.
- **[Vikunja](https://vikunja.io/)** — tasks, lists, kanban boards, and project planning.
- **[OpenProject](https://www.openproject.org/)** — project management for teams and organizations.

Use Joplin/Logseq/Notesnook for personal knowledge, LibreOffice for local documents, and Vikunja or OpenProject for structured task/project workflows.
