# Photo management

- **[Immich](https://immich.app/)** — self-hosted photo and video backup with mobile apps and timeline browsing.
- **[PhotoPrism](https://www.photoprism.app/)** — self-hosted photo library with search, albums, and metadata tools.
- **[LibrePhotos](https://docs.librephotos.com/)** — self-hosted photo management with automatic organization features.
- **[digiKam](https://www.digikam.org/)** — desktop photo organizer and editor for large local libraries.

Immich is the closest fit for a Google Photos-style personal server; digiKam is strongest when the library stays primarily on a desktop.
