# Social networks

Open-source alternatives to large centralized social platforms.

- **[Mastodon](https://joinmastodon.org/)** — Twitter/X-style microblogging built on ActivityPub. **Federated · Self-hostable**
- **[Lemmy](https://join-lemmy.org/)** — Reddit-style link aggregation and communities. **Federated · Self-hostable**
- **[PieFed](https://piefed.social/)** — lightweight federated Reddit-style communities with ActivityPub support. **Federated · Self-hostable**
- **[Pixelfed](https://pixelfed.org/)** — Instagram-style photo sharing on the Fediverse. **Federated · Self-hostable**

## Pick quickly

Choose Mastodon for short posts, Lemmy or PieFed for topic communities, and Pixelfed for photo-first sharing.
