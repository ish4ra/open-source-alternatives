# Video calls

- **[Jitsi Meet](https://jitsi.org/jitsi-meet/)** — browser-based video meetings with self-hosting support.
- **[MiroTalk](https://mirotalk.com/)** — open-source browser video meetings with several deployment modes.
- **[Nextcloud Talk](https://nextcloud.com/talk/)** — chat and video meetings integrated into Nextcloud.
- **[Galene](https://galene.org/)** — lightweight video-conferencing server designed for modest hardware.

Jitsi is the best-known general alternative to Zoom-style meetings; Nextcloud Talk fits teams already using Nextcloud.
