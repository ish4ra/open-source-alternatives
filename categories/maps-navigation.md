# Maps and navigation

Open-source alternatives to Google Maps, Apple Maps, and proprietary navigation apps.

- **[Organic Maps](https://organicmaps.app/)** — privacy-focused offline maps and navigation based on OpenStreetMap data.
- **[OsmAnd](https://osmand.net/)** — feature-rich offline navigation with routing, POIs, contour lines, GPX support, and map editing.
- **[CoMaps](https://www.comaps.app/)** — community-driven privacy-focused maps and navigation app based on OpenStreetMap.
- **[QGIS](https://qgis.org/)** — professional open-source GIS software for advanced mapping and geospatial analysis.

For everyday offline navigation, Organic Maps is the simplest choice; OsmAnd offers more advanced controls and map features.
