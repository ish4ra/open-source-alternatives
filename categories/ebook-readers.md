# E-books and reading

- **[calibre](https://calibre-ebook.com/)** — e-book library management, conversion, editing, and device transfer.
- **[KOReader](https://koreader.rocks/)** — highly configurable reader for e-ink devices, Android, and Linux.
- **[Foliate](https://johnfactotum.github.io/foliate/)** — modern desktop e-book reader for Linux.

calibre is the best library manager; KOReader is ideal for reading-focused devices and power users.
