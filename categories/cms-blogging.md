# CMS and blogging

- **[WordPress](https://wordpress.org/)** — mature publishing and CMS platform with a huge theme/plugin ecosystem.
- **[Ghost](https://ghost.org/)** — publishing platform focused on newsletters, blogs, and memberships.
- **[WriteFreely](https://writefreely.org/)** — minimalist federated blogging platform.
- **[Hugo](https://gohugo.io/)** — fast static-site generator for blogs and documentation.

WordPress is the broadest CMS; Ghost and WriteFreely focus on publishing; Hugo is ideal when a static site is enough.
