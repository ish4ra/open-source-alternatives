# Mind mapping

- **[Freeplane](https://www.freeplane.org/)** — mature mind-mapping and knowledge organization application.
- **[FreeMind](https://freemind.sourceforge.io/)** — classic Java-based mind-mapping tool.
- **[draw.io / diagrams.net](https://www.diagrams.net/)** — flexible diagramming that also works well for visual brainstorming.

Freeplane is the strongest dedicated open-source mind-map option for most desktop users.
