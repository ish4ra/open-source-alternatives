# Authenticators and 2FA

Open-source authenticator apps are especially valuable because your 2FA secrets are sensitive and portability matters.

- **[Ente Auth](https://ente.io/auth/)** — cross-platform authenticator with end-to-end encrypted backups and multi-device sync. It can also work offline and without an account if you do not want cloud backup.
- **[Aegis Authenticator](https://getaegis.app/)** — Android-focused authenticator with an encrypted local vault, strong import/export options, biometric protection, and a simple offline-first model.

## Which one should you pick?

- Choose **Ente Auth** when you want encrypted sync across multiple devices and platforms.
- Choose **Aegis** when you want a mature Android-first authenticator with a local encrypted vault and manual backup control.

Whatever app you use, export an encrypted backup and store recovery codes separately. A synced authenticator is convenient, but recovery codes are still your last-resort path when every device is unavailable.
