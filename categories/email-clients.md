# Email clients

Open-source alternatives to proprietary desktop and mobile email applications.

- **[Thunderbird](https://www.thunderbird.net/)** — mature cross-platform email, calendar, contacts, and feed client.
- **[K-9 Mail](https://k9mail.app/)** — open-source Android email client maintained by the Thunderbird project.
- **[FairEmail](https://email.faircode.eu/)** — privacy-focused Android mail client with extensive controls and protocol support.
- **[Geary](https://wiki.gnome.org/Apps/Geary)** — lightweight GNOME email client designed for simple conversation-style mail.

Thunderbird is the best general desktop choice; K-9 Mail is a strong Android option.
