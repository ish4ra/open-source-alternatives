# Image editing

- **[GIMP](https://www.gimp.org/)** — raster image editing and compositing.
- **[Krita](https://krita.org/)** — digital painting, illustration, and concept art.
- **[darktable](https://www.darktable.org/)** — RAW photo workflow and non-destructive editing.
- **[RawTherapee](https://rawtherapee.com/)** — advanced RAW photo processing.

GIMP is the broad Photoshop-style editor; Krita is strongest for painting; darktable and RawTherapee target photography workflows.
