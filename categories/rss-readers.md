# RSS readers

- **[FreshRSS](https://freshrss.org/)** — self-hosted web feed reader with multi-user support.
- **[Miniflux](https://miniflux.app/)** — minimal self-hosted feed reader focused on speed and simplicity.
- **[NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com/)** — native feed reader for Apple platforms.
- **[Feeder](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.nononsenseapps.feeder/)** — Android feed reader available through F-Droid.

FreshRSS is a strong all-round self-hosted choice; Miniflux is ideal if you prefer a simpler interface.
