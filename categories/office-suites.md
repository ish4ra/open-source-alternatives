# Office suites

Open-source alternatives to Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace for documents, spreadsheets, and presentations.

- **[LibreOffice](https://www.libreoffice.org/)** — mature desktop office suite for documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and more.
- **[ONLYOFFICE](https://www.onlyoffice.com/)** — desktop and self-hostable collaborative office suite with strong Microsoft Office format compatibility.
- **[Collabora Online](https://www.collaboraonline.com/)** — browser-based collaborative office suite built on LibreOffice technology.
- **[CryptPad](https://cryptpad.org/)** — privacy-focused collaborative office tools with client-side encryption.

LibreOffice is the strongest general desktop replacement; ONLYOFFICE is often preferable when document-format compatibility is the priority.
