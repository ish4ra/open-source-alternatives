# Screen recording and streaming

- **[OBS Studio](https://obsproject.com/)** — recording, live production, scene composition, and streaming.
- **[ShareX](https://getsharex.com/)** — Windows screenshots, screen capture, and workflow automation.
- **[Kooha](https://flathub.org/apps/io.github.seadve.Kooha)** — simple Linux screen recorder with a minimal interface.

OBS Studio is the best general-purpose choice when you need multiple sources, scenes, or streaming.
