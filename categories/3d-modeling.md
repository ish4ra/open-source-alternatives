# 3D modeling

- **[Blender](https://www.blender.org/)** — modeling, sculpting, animation, rendering, compositing, and video tools.
- **[FreeCAD](https://www.freecad.org/)** — parametric 3D CAD for engineering and product design.
- **[OpenSCAD](https://openscad.org/)** — script-based solid modeling for precise procedural designs.

Blender is the broad creative suite, FreeCAD is the engineering-oriented choice, and OpenSCAD is ideal when models are easier to define in code.
