# Video platforms

- **[PeerTube](https://joinpeertube.org/)** — federated video hosting and publishing built around ActivityPub. **Federated · Self-hostable**
- **[Owncast](https://owncast.online/)** — self-hosted live video and chat, useful as a Twitch-style alternative for creators. **Self-hosted**
- **[MediaCMS](https://mediacms.io/)** — self-hosted video and media publishing platform. **Self-hosted**

PeerTube is the strongest general YouTube-style option; Owncast is focused on live streaming rather than a full video catalog.
