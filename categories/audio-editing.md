# Audio editing

- **[Audacity](https://www.audacityteam.org/)** — waveform recording and editing for podcasts, voice, and general audio work.
- **[Ardour](https://ardour.org/)** — digital audio workstation for multitrack recording, mixing, and production.
- **[Tenacity](https://tenacityaudio.org/)** — community-developed audio editor in the Audacity family.

Audacity/Tenacity are good for straightforward editing; Ardour is the stronger DAW-style option.
